The mission

About IronMed

The IronMed 5K is HOYA Clinic's largest annual fundraiser, organized with the IronMed leadership team. It brings together medical students, faculty, alumni, health care professionals, and the wider D.C. community in support of the clinic's mission: providing free health care to underserved populations in Washington, D.C.

  • Free medical services for the clinic's patients.
  • Community health fairs and outreach across the District.
  • Partnerships that advance health equity in D.C.
